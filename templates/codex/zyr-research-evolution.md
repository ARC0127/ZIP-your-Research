# ZYR Research Evolution

Read `references/resource_policy.md` unless resource profile v1 is already
loaded through another ZYR entry. Preserve the baseline and
state the observable change before editing. Apply the user's maintenance
authorization; ordinary maintenance does not start a generated-memory workflow.

Choose validation by the actual claim:

- **Packaging/maintenance:** preserve source modules and metadata, validate
  references/build/routing/install closure, and keep an executable rollback.
  Record file-size changes as bytes, not token savings. Structural success
  establishes packaging and implemented routing behavior only.
- **Agent behavior:** use fixed baseline/candidate tasks, model/settings, and
  acceptance criteria. Measure completion quality, tools, worker use, tokens,
  and time when telemetry is available. Missing measurements are UNKNOWN.
- **Scientific capability:** read `references/full_evolution_protocol.md` for
  disjoint holdouts, mutation-sensitive evidence, counterfactuals, and the
  applicable promotion gate. Do not declare scientific improvement from builds
  or self-reported labels. Keep the existing evaluation scripts and bindings.

Complete the authorized task and relevant checks; repeat only for new changes,
failures, or unresolved concerns. Do not launch S660 for routine maintenance.
Explicitly requested multi-agent research retains the S660 contract. Persistent
generated Skill activation retains all S661 approval and host-attestation gates.

For routing use `scripts/route_manifest.py`. Historical evaluation scripts,
policy bindings, and protocols remain available for their applicable workflows.
